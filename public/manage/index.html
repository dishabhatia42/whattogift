Affiliate IDs

Every link out of Serial Gifter is checked against this list. A link to a shop listed here gets your ID added (for example Amazon's tag=); a link to any other shop gets Serial Gifter's tracking tags (utm_source=serialgifter) so the brand sees the visits you send in its own analytics. Changes reach the site within a minute.

The shop's affiliate programme tells you the parameter name and your ID. Amazon: tag. Many Shopify brand programmes use ref or sca_ref.
